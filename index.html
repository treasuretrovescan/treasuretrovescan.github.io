    Selling Guide

    Easy steps. Plain words. This is how good sellers do it.

    Tap a big button:

    Pick good groups

    • Pick groups with rules and with leaders who watch them. Bad groups are full of scams.
    • No Rule Card yet? Read the group's rules first — or let us do it in your setup.
    • Good search words: “silver buy sell trade”, “coin buy sell trade”.
    • Private groups: we do not support them. Locked groups hide their rules and posts, and we never ask for your password. See the FAQ page for the full why.

    We read the group rules for you

    • Every group has its own rules. Reading them all takes hours. You do not have to.
    • When you get set up with us, we read the rules of YOUR groups for you.
    • You get a simple Rule Card for each of your groups, in plain words: how sales end, what photos they want, how you can take pay, and how your post must look so it does not get taken down.
    • Your Rule Cards come in your pack email. Keep them on your phone. Look before you post. That is it.

    Sample Rule Card — "Silver Stackers BST":
    Sales end 24 hours after the LAST bid. Photos: your own, with name + date paper. Pay: PayPal or Venmo Goods & Services only. Post must start with year + what it is.

    Take good photos — the light box way

    A light box is a small white box with soft lights inside. It makes a coin look bright and clean, with no dark shadows. Good photos sell faster — and for more money.

    • Best way: a small light box (they are cheap online). Coin on a little clear stand inside. Phone in front. Tap to focus. Snap it.
    • No light box yet? Curve a big white paper up a wall for your background, sit by a bright window in the daytime, and turn the flash OFF. Daylight is free and it works.
    • Front and back, every time. Fill the whole photo with the coin — big, not tiny.
    • Put the coin next to a paper with your name and today's date on it. That proves it is yours.
    • Show any marks or damage. Tell the truth. Buyers trust you when you do.
    • Do not clean a coin. Cleaning makes it worth less.

    Price it fair

    • First, know the melt price. That is the floor. Do not go under it.
    • Look at sold prices. Not wish prices.
    • Use our Price Tool. It adds your costs so you do not lose money.
    • No bids at your price? Wrong group, or wrong time. That is why the Radar helps.

    The right words sell it

    • First line of your post: say what it is. Year. Weight. What it is made of. Buyers decide in two seconds.
    • Bad words: "Nice coin!! Best price!!!" — nobody trusts yelling.
    • Good words: "2021 Silver Libertad, 1 oz, .999 silver. $85 shipped. I combine shipping." Short. True. Clear.
    • We write yours for you. Your pack comes with your post words and your winner notes, filled in with your pay apps and your shipping prices. You just copy, paste, post.

    Get paid safe

    • Use PayPal Goods & Services, or Venmo Goods & Services. They protect both sides.
    • Friends & Family does not protect you. Scammers love to ask for it.
    • Zelle can not be taken back. Use it only with people you know.
    • Do not ship till the money is in. Never pay back an “extra” payment. That is a trick.
    • If a buyer is in a big rush, slow down. Real buyers wait.

    Ship it right

    • Wrap each coin so it can not move or rub.
    • Use a strong padded mailer or a small box.
    • Always use tracking. Add insurance for big sales.
    • Take a photo of the pack job. Save your chats till the buyer gets it.
    • Send the buyer the tracking number. Say thank you.

    Trust is the real money

    • Help in the group. Answer questions. Do not just sell.
    • Ship fast. Price fair. Tell the truth.
    • People remember your name. Good names get good bids.
